About Me

The person behind the work

I spent 4.5 years at ZoomRx Healthcare Technologies doing what most people are only starting to learn: running AI-powered workflows in production. I designed LLM prompt frameworks, structured unstructured data for AI pipelines, evaluated AI output quality end-to-end, and traced errors in AI systems to identify where outputs broke down — while simultaneously owning GTM execution for an AI SaaS platform.

I built a 19,000+ prospect database using Clay and LinkedIn Sales Navigator, ran multi-channel outreach via Brevo and GMass achieving ~50% open rates, and acted as the bridge between product, data, and engineering to ship AI features that solved real user problems.

I specialise in AI workflow operations — designing prompts, evaluating output quality, and building automation systems that connect AI to real business outcomes. I know what good looks like and how to get there.
